Hi, I'm Lin 👋

Understanding how cities take shape — and why it matters.

理解城市如何形成，以及它为何重要。

I am a Lecturer at the School of Earth Sciences, Yunnan University. 现任云南大学地球科学学院讲师。

I study how urban spatial systems form and evolve, and how their spatial structures shape sustainability and equity. 我的研究关注城市空间系统如何形成与演化， 以及空间结构如何影响城市的可持续性与社会公平。

My work connects urban geography with spatial data, complex networks, remote sensing, and GeoAI — not as ends in themselves, but as tools for understanding real urban questions. 我将城市地理与空间数据、复杂网络、遥感和 GeoAI 相结合， 并将这些方法作为理解真实城市问题的工具。

Research 研究方向

01 · How do cities take shape?

城市是如何形成和演化的？

How do urban form, road networks, land use, infrastructure, and spatial interactions shape the structure and evolution of cities?

城市形态、道路网络、土地利用、基础设施与空间相互作用 如何共同塑造城市空间结构及其演化？

Urban Form Road Networks Land Use Complex Systems
02 · Does urban form shape sustainability?

城市空间结构如何影响可持续性？

How do spatial structures influence heat, carbon emissions, mobility, resource efficiency, and urban resilience?

城市空间结构如何影响热环境、碳排放、 居民出行、资源效率与城市韧性？

Urban Climate Carbon Mobility Resilience
03 · Who benefits, and who bears the risks?

城市发展的收益与风险由谁承担？

How are environmental risks, public resources, and opportunities distributed across places and people?

环境风险、公共资源与发展机会 如何在不同空间与社会群体之间分配？

Spatial Equity Accessibility Environmental Exposure Spatial Justice
