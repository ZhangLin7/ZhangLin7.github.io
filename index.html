Understanding how cities take shape — and why it matters.

Using spatiotemporal data and GeoAI to study sustainability and spatial equity in cities of the Global South.

以时空数据和 GeoAI 研究全球南方城市的可持续发展与空间公平。

I focus on how different spatial structures shape the distribution of environmental risks and public services within cities.

关注于不同空间结构如何影响环境风险和公共服务在城市中的分配。

Research 研究方向

01 · How do cities take shape?
城市是如何形成和演化的？

Understanding how urban form, road networks, land use and spatial interactions shape the structure and evolution of cities.

Urban Form Road Networks Complex Systems
02 · Does urban form shape sustainability?
城市空间结构如何影响可持续性？

Exploring how spatial structures influence urban climate, carbon emissions, mobility and resilience.

Urban Climate Carbon Resilience
03 · Who benefits, and who bears the risks?
城市发展的收益与风险由谁承担？

Studying how environmental risks, public resources and opportunities are distributed across places and people.

Spatial Equity Accessibility Spatial Justice
